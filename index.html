BEST SCENERY

Taum Sauk → Johnson’s Shut-Ins

Use a selected portion of the Taum Sauk section for a two-day point-to-point. Expect rocky footing, steep changes and limited trail marking in places.

Suggested mileage16–20 miles
Full sectionAbout 35 miles
FormatPoint-to-point
DifficultyHard

Highlights: Mina Sauk Falls, Missouri high point, Devil’s Tollgate, rhyolite ridges and fall color.

Water: Seasonal creeks; carry enough to bridge dry stretches and filter all natural water.

🐕 Dogs allowed: Yes. In Missouri state parks, keep dogs supervised on a leash no longer than 10 ft. Dogs cannot use the trail into the main Johnson’s Shut-Ins swimming area.

BEST SIMPLE LOOP

Bell Mountain Loop

The cleanest two-day option because it starts and ends at the same trailhead. Rugged enough to feel worthwhile without arranging a shuttle.

Mileage11.9 miles
FormatLoop
DifficultyModerate
Best seasonFall

Tradeoff: Shorter than your usual 8–10 miles per day. You can slow down, explore, photograph and camp near the ridge.

🐕 Dogs allowed: Yes. The Forest Service says dogs are allowed on hiking trails and should be under control; leashing is encouraged. Pack out waste or bury it at least 100 ft from trails and water.

BEST BASECAMP

Johnson’s Shut-Ins / Goggins Mountain

Best when you want a state-park campground and a roughly 11-mile backcountry loop without moving vehicles.

MileageAbout 10.9 miles
FormatLoop
DifficultyModerate
BonusShut-ins visit

Tradeoff: Less dramatic than the strongest Taum Sauk segment, but easier logistically and better for a relaxed meetup.

🐕 Dogs allowed: Yes, on the backcountry trail. Keep dogs supervised on a leash no longer than 10 ft. Pets are prohibited on the separate trail leading into the shut-ins swimming area.
